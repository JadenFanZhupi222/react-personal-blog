# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Visitors are developers, recruiters, collaborators, and friends who want to understand Shijie Fan / Zhupi222 through technical writing, projects, activity data, and personal interests. They arrive to scan competence quickly, then dive into articles, projects, or Steam achievement details when something catches their attention.

## Product Purpose

This site is a personal blog and portfolio. It should present technical ability, taste, and personality in one place: a cinematic entry page, a useful home dashboard, readable blog posts, project proof, and game/activity signals. Success means the site feels memorable and technically capable while remaining fast, navigable, and comfortable to read.

## Brand Personality

Technical, cinematic, playful. The interface should feel like a developer's personal command center with moments of visual craft, not a generic startup template. Game and achievement details can add personality, but the main identity is software craft.

## Anti-references

Avoid generic AI-purple landing pages, endless glass cards, noisy animation on reading surfaces, template-style equal card grids, and motion that fights content. Do not turn blog articles into a visual demo page. Avoid game UI cosplay across the whole site; keep Steam/achievement energy localized.

## Design Principles

1. Show craft through real interaction, not decorative clutter.
2. Make the entrance and home pages memorable, then let content pages breathe.
3. Keep motion purposeful: reveal hierarchy, signal interactivity, or express achievement state.
4. Preserve reading comfort on blog pages and scanning comfort on list pages.
5. Respect reduced motion and lower-power devices.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG AA contrast for text and controls. Every substantial animation needs a reduced-motion path. Effects should degrade cleanly when WebGL, pointer precision, or device performance is limited. Navigation, language switching, theme switching, and article reading must remain keyboard-accessible.
